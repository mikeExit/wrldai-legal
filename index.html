WrldAI Instagram Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

This policy covers the WrldAI Instagram Publisher, a private internal tool used by its operator to publish content to their own Instagram Business account. It is not a public service and has no end users other than the account owner.

What the app accesses

Through the Instagram Graph API, and only for the connected account:

  • Basic account information — account id, username and account type (permission: instagram_business_basic). Used to confirm the connection is valid before publishing.
  • Content publishing — uploads and publishes video (Reels) that the operator has created and explicitly approved (permission: instagram_business_content_publish).
  • Metrics on its own posts — aggregate like and comment counts, timestamp and permalink, used to review the performance of content it published.

What the app stores

  • A long-lived access token and the Instagram user id, held in a local environment file on the operator's own machine. Nothing is stored on a hosted server, shared database or third-party service.
  • Nothing else. The app does not collect, store or process personal data belonging to any other Instagram user, follower or commenter. It reads only aggregate counts on its own posts, never individual identities.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or transferred to any third party. The application contains no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Retention and revocation

The access token expires after 60 days unless refreshed. Access can be revoked at any time from Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites, which immediately and permanently ends the application's access.
